# Retell AI — Persona Decisions

## Decision frame

- **Company:** Retell AI
- **Research cutoff:** 2026-09-06
- **Geography:** Global, with United States-specific regulatory treatment where relevant
- **Purpose:** Decide how many role-level dossiers the public evidence supports before any persona files are written
- **Rule:** A candidate becomes a standalone persona only when at least two of these are materially distinct and publicly supported: authority, jobs and metrics, objections and proof needs, lifecycle ownership, or repeated appearance in named customer evidence.
- **Result:** Eleven candidate roles were tested. Seven are standalone, three are merged, and one is index-only. The resulting count happens to be seven; it was not inherited from Otera.

## Why the seven standalone roles remain separate

### Executive Sponsor versus Workflow Owner

The same person can hold both roles in a small company, but the jobs separate at scale. Carter Li at SWTCH ties the program to support cost and SaaS margin; Mike Tadlock at Pine Park ties it to provider capacity, patient access and revenue per provider day. A functional owner has to define queues, scripts, handoffs, schedules and frontline change. The sponsor decides whether the financial and strategic result justifies exposure and expansion.

### Technical Owner versus Agent Builder

Retell itself encodes this boundary imperfectly but visibly. The current [Access Control page](https://docs.retellai.com/accounts/access-control) is internally inconsistent: its introduction names Admin, Developer, Analyst, and Viewer, while its body describes Admin, Developer, and Member. The specific documented permission boundary still matters—broader administration versus building/testing/log/key work—but the labels are not a stable exhaustive organizational taxonomy. Named cases also distinguish architecture teams from day-to-day builders. The technical owner chooses and governs the production pattern; the builder works inside it.

### Agent Builder versus Conversation Quality Owner

Building a graph or prompt is not the same as operating conversation quality. Matic assigns a dedicated team member to review more than 150 calls a week and also uses automated transcript checks. Pine Park trained Medical Assistants to review transcripts and handle time-sensitive exceptions. Spare reports more than 500 evaluation flows. These are recurring production-operations jobs with regression, sampling and outcome metrics, not a one-time implementation task.

### Governance Approver versus Technical Owner

The technical owner can demonstrate encryption, data flows, failure handling and access boundaries. Security, privacy, legal, compliance and procurement decide whether those controls and contract terms meet organizational and jurisdictional requirements. Retell's public [Terms](https://www.retellai.com/legal/terms-of-service) place important duties on the customer, while its [Compliance page](https://docs.retellai.com/general/compliance) describes DPA/SCC transfer mechanisms and separately says services are not currently operated in an EU region. Those are different questions—lawful transfer mechanisms do not equal physical residency. Governance remains standalone because it has blocking authority, distinct legal/security/commercial proof needs, and approval/change-control ownership.

### Solution Partner & Platform Operator versus a direct customer

Partner economics and operating scope differ materially. ISpeedToLead used a certified partner with no internal engineering; Mindcraft Labs integrated Everise. Cents and Spare embed Retell into products delivered to thousands of downstream businesses or public agencies. These operators need reusable configuration, APIs, bulk changes, client isolation, support leverage and viable per-client margin. A single-enterprise workflow owner does not own those jobs.

### Why partner delivery and embedded platforms share one dossier

Before final counting, agency/reseller/BPO delivery and vertical-SaaS/OEM operation were tested as separate submotions. Both have a recurring multi-client job: standardize configurations, isolate customers, manage shared upstream change, control support effort, and protect margin while downstream workflows vary. Their commercial packaging, contract chain, account ownership, and degree of product embedding differ, so the dossier keeps them as explicit variants rather than flattening them. The default public [Terms](https://www.retellai.com/legal/terms-of-service) restrict certain resale, standalone voice-output, substitute, and intermediary uses; observed partner or embedded deployments do not prove that default terms authorize them. Applicable partner/platform/order-form rights must be confirmed in writing.

## Segment variants, not extra personas

The Workflow Owner dossier must explicitly cover two variants:

1. **Service and operations:** containment, response time, abandonment, cost per resolution, escalation quality, customer satisfaction, collections and capacity.
2. **Revenue and admissions:** speed-to-lead, pickup, qualification, meeting or trial booking, conversion, revenue per lead and human closer utilization.

The Executive Sponsor dossier must cover both smaller-company founder/CEO concentration and enterprise separation among COO, CIO or transformation leadership. The Governance Approver dossier must change proof by jurisdiction and regulated use case rather than inventing a separate healthcare, finance or public-sector persona.

## Evidence limitations affecting the decisions

- Retell's published customer stories are selected by the vendor and usually do not reveal the full buying committee.
- Named speakers skew senior. Public evidence is weaker for mid-level operations, finance, procurement, privacy and security titles.
- G2 and Trustpilot review counts and summaries change rapidly; many reviews are anonymous, invited or incentivized. They support workflow-language hypotheses, not role prevalence.
- Product workspace roles describe permissions, not organizational titles. They cannot by themselves prove a persona.
- No CRM, win/loss, customer-call, renewal, procurement or implementation-retrospective data was available.
- One person can perform several of these roles, especially in an SMB or agency. The dossiers represent jobs and decision authority, not mandatory seats in every account.
