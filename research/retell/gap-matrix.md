## Unresolved diligence list

1. Audited revenue, revenue-recognition basis, gross margin, burn, cash balance, and profitability period.
2. Current cap table, total capital raised, latest post-money valuation, and investor rights.
3. Gross and net revenue retention, logo churn, expansion by cohort, and share of customers still in pilot.
4. Representative quality metrics by workflow: containment, false completion, hallucination, transfer, repeat contact, and human QA burden.
5. Enterprise contract price, minimums, support commitments, remedies, and actual SLA versus public terms.
6. Independent security/audit artifacts, data locations, model-provider data handling, and subprocessor flow.
7. Customer mix by revenue, concentration, industry, geography, and direct customer versus agency/reseller.
8. Exact current headcount and organization shape; credible employee-experience evidence remains insufficient.
9. Defensible market share. No public denominator and no verified company volume share are available.
10. Retell-specific legal claims, enforcement, breach history, or material disputes should be re-searched before external publication.
