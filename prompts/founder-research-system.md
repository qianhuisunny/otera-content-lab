# About the Founder

## 1. Research Sources & Methods

### Primary Sources

- Podcast appearances (especially longer-form interviews)
- Conference presentations and Q&A sessions
- Written articles or blog posts (personal, not just company content)
- Social media activity across platforms
- Company all-hands recordings or internal communications (if available)

### Secondary Sources

- Employee reviews mentioning leadership style
- Customer testimonials that reference founder interactions
- Industry peer commentary or competitive analysis
- Media coverage and journalist observations
- Board member or investor perspectives (if publicly available)

### Observational Research

- How do they behave in unscripted moments? Q&A sessions, informal conversation?
- What topics make them light up versus what feels obligatory?
- How consistent is their message across different audiences and contexts?
- What do they choose to share versus keep private? What does this boundary reveal?

## 2. Communication Patterns & Quirks

### Signature Phrases & Concepts

- What words or phrases do they use repeatedly?
- What’s their verbal signature?
- How do they explain complex concepts? What frameworks or models do they default to?
- What clichés do they avoid? What business-speak makes them cringe?
- How do they structure arguments or presentations? Linear, circular, story-driven?

### Authentic Voice Indicators

- When do they sound most natural and energetic versus scripted?
- What topics make them forget to use business language and just talk like themselves?
- How does their communication style change between formal and informal settings?
- What communication style do they use when they’re frustrated or passionate?

## 3. Red Flags & Blind Spots

### Potential Inconsistencies

- Are there gaps between what they say publicly and how the company actually operates?
- Do they have any hot-button topics where they become defensive or lose their usual calm?
- Are there areas where their personal brand conflicts with their company brand?
- What criticism do they consistently deflect rather than address?

### Growth Challenges

- What aspects of their original vision might not scale with company growth?
- Where might their personal communication style clash with broader market needs?
- What founder-specific quirks might need to be softened for wider audience appeal?

## 4. Personal Background & Context

### Formative Experiences

- Where did they grow up? What socioeconomic context shaped their worldview?
- Early career experiences? Military, corporate, entrepreneurial?
- Any significant personal challenges they’ve overcome?
- Educational background — formal training versus self-taught? How do they value each?

### Current Life Context

- Family situation? How do they balance personal and professional priorities?
- Hobbies or interests outside work? What do these reveal about personality?
- How do they recharge or find inspiration?
- Who’s in their personal advisory circle? What kinds of people do they seek counsel from?

## 5. Industry Perspective & Vision

### Market Philosophy

- How do they view their industry’s current state? Broken, evolving, needing reinvention?
- What conventional wisdom do they challenge?
- Who do they see as the real competition — direct competitors or alternative solutions?
- How do they predict their industry will evolve? What role will they play in this shift?

### Innovation Approach

- Are they first-mover types or fast-followers who perfect existing concepts?
- How do they balance innovation with proven business models?
- Relationship with emerging technologies — early adopter or thoughtful skeptic?
- How do they think about customer education versus meeting customers where they are?

## 6. Customer & Market Relationship

### Customer Philosophy

- How do they talk about their customers — partners, users, clients, community?
- What customer feedback do they value most? What do they dismiss or deprioritize?
- How hands-on are they with customer interactions? Do they still take sales calls?
- What customer success stories do they tell most often? What does this reveal?

### Market Positioning

- How do they position their company against competitors?
- What market dynamics frustrate them most?
- Where do they see opportunity?
- How do they balance serving current customers versus attracting new markets?

## 7. Professional Background & Journey

### Career Evolution

- What was their path to founding this company? Linear progression or pivotal shift?
- What roles did they hold before founding? What skills did those roles develop?
- Which experiences do they reference most often in interviews or talks?
- What failures or setbacks shaped their approach?
- Who were their mentors or key influences? What philosophies did they absorb?

### Founding Story

- What specific problem were they experiencing that led to starting the company?
- Was it a “Eureka moment” or a gradual realization?
- What sacrifices did they make to get started?
- How do they frame those decisions?
- Who were early team members or co-founders?
- What was their deeper “why” beyond business opportunity?

## 8. Communication Style & Personality

### Public Speaking & Writing

- How do they present at conferences/interviews? Formal, casual, technical?
- What’s their natural communication rhythm — quick & energetic or thoughtful?
- Do they use humor? What kind?
- How technical do they get when explaining their business?
- What metaphors or analogies do they favor?

### Social Media & Digital Presence

- Which platforms do they use most actively?
- How does their voice differ across platforms?
- What kinds of topics do they post about — industry insights, personal moments, company updates?
- How do they engage with critics or disagreements — defensive, open, combative?
- What topics make them most passionate or animated?

### Media Interactions

- How do they handle tough questions? Deflect, tackle head-on, redirect?
- What topics generate their strongest energy?
- Do they stick to talking points or go off-script?
- How do they handle criticism of their company or industry?

## 9. Values & Philosophy

### Core Beliefs

- What principles do they reference repeatedly across different contexts?
- How do they talk about success — money, impact, team growth, personal fulfillment?
- What industry norms do they criticize? What alternative do they propose?
- How do they view competition — collaborative, zero-sum, ignore-and-focus?
- What role does technology play in their worldview?

### Decision-Making Philosophy

- Are they data-driven or intuition-driven?
- How do they describe their decision process?
- How do they handle uncertainty or ambiguity?
- What’s their risk tolerance?
- How do they talk about big bets or failures?
- Do they prioritize speed or perfection?
- How do they balance consensus vs decisive leadership?

### Personal Mission

- What change do they want to see in their industry or the world?
- How do they define company impact beyond financial metrics?
- What legacy do they want to build?
- What problems beyond business do they care about?

## 10. Leadership Style & Team Philosophy

### People Management

- How do they talk about their team?
- What language do they use to describe employees?
- What qualities do they prioritize when hiring?
- How do they handle public credit for successes — solo credit or team?
- What’s their approach to work-life balance?
- How do they discuss company culture?
- What behaviors or attitudes do they model?

### Growth & Scaling

- How do they maintain culture during hyper-growth?
- What aspects of “startup life” do they want to preserve vs refine?
- How do they think about delegation?
- What do they struggle to let go of?
- What keeps them up at night as the company grows?
