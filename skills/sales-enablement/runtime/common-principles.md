# Shared Sales Enablement Guidance

## Before Starting

Use the supplied product-marketing context before asking questions. Ask only for
information that is missing or specific to this deal.

Confirm the following context:

1. **Value Proposition & Differentiators**
   - What is being sold and who is it for?
   - What makes it different from the next best alternative?
   - Which outcomes can be supported with evidence?
2. **Sales Motion**
   - What is the sales stage and motion?
   - Who participates in the buying decision?
   - Who will present and reuse the material?
3. **Buyer Situation**
   - Which persona, problem, use case, and desired next step matter now?
   - Which objections or decision risks must the material address?
4. **Current State**
   - Which relevant materials and proof already exist?
   - What is working, missing, stale, or disputed?

## Core Principles

### Sales Uses What Sales Trusts

Use the language sales teams and buyers use. Prefer evidence that a rep can
defend, and keep unsupported claims visibly qualified.

### Situation-Specific, Not Generic

Tailor the material to the persona, deal stage, and use case. Different buyers
need different emphasis, depth, and proof.

### Scannable Over Comprehensive

Make the main point easy to find in seconds. Use short statements, clear visual
hierarchy, and only the detail needed for the sales conversation.

### Tie Back to Business Outcomes

Connect every material claim to revenue, efficiency, or risk reduction. State
the buyer consequence rather than leaving a feature to explain itself.
